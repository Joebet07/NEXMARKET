Shipping, made clear.

These are sample policies for this academic demonstration. No actual shipments are created.

Delivery options

Sample standard delivery costs ₱150 per order. Illustrative delivery times are 3–5 business days within Metro Manila and 5–7 business days elsewhere in the Philippines. Actual rates, methods and coverage would need confirmation before a real launch.

Carefully packed

In a real marketplace, electronics should use protective packaging, cushioning and securely sealed boxes. Any included cables and accessories should be listed clearly.

Order tracking

Demonstration checkout creates a local order reference only. It is not a courier tracking number. A production service would provide a separate tracking link once an item is dispatched.

Before accepting a parcel

For a real purchase, inspect the packaging, verify the item against your order and document any visible damage promptly. Contact the actual seller or courier using their verified support details.
