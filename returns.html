A fair second look.

Illustrative return policy only. NEXTMARKET does not process real orders, refunds or returns.

Sample eligibility

For this concept, a buyer could request a return within 7 calendar days of receipt if the item is materially different from its description, damaged on arrival or defective. Keep the packaging and all included accessories.

Brand-new items

Sample returns would require the device, original accessories and proof of purchase. Any activation locks or personal accounts would need to be removed before a device is returned.

Pre-loved items

Used items would be assessed against their stated condition grade and disclosed defects. Accurately described wear would not, by itself, qualify as a mismatch under this example policy.

How a return would work

Contact the seller with an order reference, explanation and relevant photos. After approval, follow the seller’s instructions. A real service would confirm responsibility for return shipping before dispatch.

Sample refund process

In a production marketplace, a refund would be reviewed after inspection and returned through the original payment method. A sample processing window is 5–10 business days after approval. No actual refunds or financial transactions happen here.

Policy limitations

These are fictional project details, not legal or commercial terms for a real business. Applicable consumer protections and verified business policies would govern actual transactions.
