Your next starts here.

NEXTMARKET is a student-built electronics marketplace concept, created to make buying and selling technology easier to understand.

A smarter way to upgrade

From a first laptop to a favorite pair of headphones, great technology can open doors. Our concept brings brand-new and pre-loved electronics together with clear conditions, useful filters and understandable price information.

Our mission

Help people discover suitable electronics and give existing devices a longer useful life through transparent product information.

Our vision

A more accessible, circular technology marketplace where informed choices make upgrades feel simple.

How it works

Browse the catalog, compare simulated sale prices and save favorites. Try adding products to the cart or submitting a demo offer. Sellers can upload images and publish local demo listings, then edit or remove them at any time.

Built for learning

This is an academic frontend project using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, vanilla JavaScript and Chart.js. NEXTMARKET is a fictional brand, not an operating business. Listings and activity stay in your browser; local demo accounts only, with no payments, seller verification or real shipping are provided.
